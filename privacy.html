Privacy Policy

ProjectViral connects to TikTok only with the permissions the account owner grants: basic profile info, video upload and publish, and reading the owner's own video list and stats.

  • What is stored: TikTok access and refresh tokens, the account's open ID, and view, like, comment and share counts for videos the app posted.
  • Where: only on the owner's own computer, in a local file. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API.
  • Sharing: no data is sold or shared with anyone.
  • Removal: the owner can disconnect at any time by revoking the app in TikTok settings and deleting the local file.
